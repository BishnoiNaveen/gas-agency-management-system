2. MAIN MENU OVERVIEW
---------------------
   1 - Customer Management    (add/view/edit customers)
   2 - Cylinder Inventory     (check and update LPG stock)
   3 - Booking Management     (create and manage orders)
   4 - Billing Management     (bills, payments, reports)
   5 - Delivery Management    (deliver cylinders to customers)
   6 - Admin Dashboard        (business summary)
   0 - Exit (Logout)

3. ADD A NEW CUSTOMER
---------------------
Menu 1 -> Option 1 -> Add Customer
   - Enter name, 10-digit phone, and full address
   - Note the Customer ID shown after saving

4. CREATE A BOOKING (ORDER)
---------------------------
Menu 3 -> Option 1 -> Create Booking
   - Enter Customer ID
   - Select cylinder type (5 KG / 14 KG / 19 KG)
   - Enter quantity
   - Stock is automatically reserved

5. DELIVER CYLINDER TO CUSTOMER  *** MAIN DELIVERY WORKFLOW ***
---------------------------------------------------------------
Menu 5 -> Delivery Management

   Step A: View Pending Deliveries (Option 1)
           Shows customer name, phone, ADDRESS, cylinder, and amount

   Step B: Deliver Cylinder to Customer (Option 2)
           - Enter Booking ID
           - Verify customer name, phone, and delivery address
           - Confirm with 'y'
           - Bill is auto-generated
           - Delivery slip saved in data\reports\delivery_slip_X.txt

6. COLLECT PAYMENT
------------------
Menu 4 -> Option 2 -> Mark Bill as Paid
   - Enter Bill ID from the delivery/billing screen

7. VIEW REPORTS
---------------
   - Dashboard (Menu 6): overall business summary
   - Revenue Report (Menu 4 -> Option 3)
   - Export Bills to CSV (Menu 4 -> Option 4)

8. DELIVERY SLIPS
-----------------
After each delivery, a slip is saved at:
   data\reports\delivery_slip_[BookingID].txt

You can print this file for your records or give to the customer.

9. TIPS
-------
   - Always add customer with correct address before booking
   - Use Delivery Management (Menu 5) for daily dispatch work
   - Check Dashboard at end of day for pending collections
   - Do not delete customers with pending bookings

10. EXIT
--------
Press 0 on main menu to logout safely.
